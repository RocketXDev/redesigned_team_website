# Leah Neset & Artem Markelov

**U.S. Ice Dance · Colorado Springs, Colorado**

*Skate with purpose. Trust the process.*

Welcome! This is the home of our official website, **[nesetmarkelov.com](https://nesetmarkelov.com)**.

We are Leah Neset and Artem Markelov, a U.S. senior ice dance team. We have skated together since 2020, became 2024 World Junior Champions, 2023 Junior Grand Prix Final Champions and three-time U.S. Junior Champions, and now compete on the ISU Grand Prix — on the road to the Olympic Games.
